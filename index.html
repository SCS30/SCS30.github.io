About

Chemist.
Inventor. Advocate.

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at Durham University working at the intersection of synthetic and computational chemistry. My PhD at York focused on Csp²–Csp³ Suzuki-Miyaura cross-couplings, and my current research spans gold-catalysed systems, in-situ ESI-MS monitoring, and data-driven mechanistic analysis across institutions in the UK and Canada.

I hold WIPO certifications in IP (DL101) and patents (DL177), and am actively building toward a career as a trainee patent attorney — translating complex chemistry into protected innovation. I am a commissioned contributor to Chemistry World (RSC) on research culture and the digitalisation of chemistry.

My background includes industrial placements at AstraZeneca and GSK, AI consultancy at Outlier, a McKinsey Forward programme, and 20+ international conference presentations.
